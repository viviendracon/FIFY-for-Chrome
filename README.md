# FIFY (Fixed It For You) for Chrome
Chrome extension that replaces dramatic headline buzzwords and all caps with measured alternatives.
- No more clapbacks, slams, etc.
- Configurable replacements allow you to expand the list of drek phrases and words you don't need or want to see anymore. 
- Lower your blood pressure and outrage to manageable levels and take back your mindspace. 

---

### How to Install It Locally

1. Download FIFY folder to your computer.
2. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** via the toggle switch in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner and select your folder.
5. To manage the dictionary, find the extensions icon in the top right-hand of your browser next to the address bar.
6. Add in your rows and substitutions and save changes.
